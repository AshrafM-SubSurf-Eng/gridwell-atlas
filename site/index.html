Which of America's abandoned oil and gas wells could be turned into Enhanced Geothermal System (EGS) wells? GRIDWELL sorts every inactive or unproductive well by how it was abandoned, estimates the rock temperature at its own depth, and measures how far it sits from a high-voltage grid connection and whether it earns the energy-community tax-credit bonus.
