# Data licence

The derived data GRIDWELL publishes (everything under `site/data/` and the
assets attached to `data-*` releases) is released under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Cite as: Mohammed, A. (2026). *GRIDWELL Atlas: Geothermal Repurposing Inventory of Disused Wells* (version and
refresh date as shown on the page). https://github.com/AshrafM-SubSurf-Eng/gridwell-atlas

The inputs keep their own terms, listed with links in `sources.json` and on the page:
state well registries are public records; the USGS Documented Unplugged Orphaned
Oil and Gas Well dataset is CC0 1.0; the Stanford Thermal Earth Model bottom-hole
temperature compilation is CC BY 4.0 (Aljubran & Horne, DOE Geothermal Data
Repository, doi:10.15121/2324793); Census, EIA, Treasury and IRS files are U.S.
Government works.
